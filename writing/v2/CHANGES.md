## Framing
- Title and lead: from "the state installs, the script does not" to "talk does not come
  apart from state easily" — the failure to build the narration-only organism, taken apart
  family by family, is the main result; the loading map is supporting evidence with a
  one-armed narration axis.
- The installability claim is stated at the strength the data support: hard and never
  reliable, **not** impossible (see below). H-optimisation vs H-entanglement named; REP02
  described as the causal test, pre-registered, harness-failed, no number.

## Claims corrected (each verified against the committed JSONs)
1. **"No organism came back contingent AND policy-invariant"** (v1 abstract, §4.1, README)
   — false. `scripts/nar_ledger.py` census over 294 ORG-B-kind organisms: 17 contingent;
   **six** kept a policy inside the invariance band with intact move entropy (E17 pool1
   s2/s4, pool1_bal s4/s5; NAR01a enum s7, enum_bal s3). In NAR02b's 32, one contingent
   organism sits inside the ratio band with a collapsed (constant-move) policy — Table J7b
   already showed 1/8. New wording: no recipe installs it reliably; in the decomposition,
   contingency arrived with the state.
2. **"~370 organisms released as LoRA adapters"** — 44 files in release
   `adapters-20260817-d085494`. Appendix Q states exactly what exists.
3. **"264 organisms" in the pool family** — 216 distinct; NAR01a's control/pool1 are
   bit-identical rebuilds of E17's (NAR01 RESULTS.md's "eight fresh seeds" is wrong).
4. **"~26 rented GPU-hours"** — predates NAR02–NAR04; committed manifests sum to
   ≈37.3 GPU-hours of job time over 69 manifests (Appendix N).
5. **RL avoidance "scale resolves (6/6 at 14B)"** — 32B is 4/6; now "improves with scale".
6. **NAR03 "presence rose to 0.96–0.99 on both classes"** — weight-5 arm is 0.78/0.78.
7. **PAP01 "no |d| exceeds 0.44 under any list"** — the committed list has I4 narration
   +0.755; now "no |d| exceeds 0.43 under either new list".
8. **NAR03b/c "29–58 GPU-min per shard"** — 37–68 from the manifests.
9. **VAL01 I2 under P-AVOID** is a null (−0.8, 4/8); the text now says so, and the
   previously EMPTY Appendix H table (a key-name bug in `appendix_tables.py`) is fixed.
10. **SCL01 "B collapse worst at 14B, 5/6"** — 5/6 was the modal-share definition and
    the 0.6B row; under the presence definition used in Table G3, 14B is 4/6.
11. **A′ lottery sd** — main text now quotes the population sd that Table G3 prints
    (0.184 → 0.014); the sample sd 0.201 → 0.016 is noted in Appendix G.
12. **PAP02 yellow contingency** — pre-registered bar 0.3: 9/12; at 0.5: 6/12; both given.
13. Appendix G notes that 14B/32B were added to the four pre-registered sizes on the day,
